Blur and Melt help
Blur averages only selected source pixels and writes inside the selection. After Deselect, Blur image works across the whole image. Melt needs a selection, reads the heights just outside it and softens only its inside edge. Outside pixels stay unchanged.
Preview first, then Apply or Cancel. An explicitly empty selection blocks both filters. Changing settings clears the preview so you can preview again.
